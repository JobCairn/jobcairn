# Backend Engineer (LLM) at Example Labs

Remote Australia · Full time · $135k-$150k + super

## About Example Labs

Example Labs builds document search for law firms. Lawyers use us to find the one clause that matters in a pile of contracts, court files and emails. Our backend serves 40 million pages and an LLM answer layer on top. We are 70 people, fully remote across Australia, and we meet in person twice a year.

## The role

Our customers upload more every month, and the biggest matters now run to hundreds of thousands of pages. We need another backend engineer to keep ingestion fast, search accurate and answers quick as we grow. You will join the platform squad of five and report to our Head of Engineering.

## What you'll do

- Design and run Node.js services for ingestion, search and answers.
- Build retrieval pipelines: parsing, chunking, embeddings and hybrid search.
- Keep latency low and costs predictable as usage grows.
- Write clear design notes before big changes, and review your teammates' notes.
- Join the on-call roster (one week in six). Pages are rare, and we fix the cause the next day.

## You have

- 4+ years of backend work in Node.js or TypeScript.
- Production experience with Postgres and queues.
- Built or run a retrieval pipeline.
- Comfort reading traces and fixing slow paths.

## Bonus

- Experience with legal or other long documents.
- Go. A few of our older services use it.

## How we work

We write things down. Most decisions start as a one-page note in our wiki, and meetings are short. Core hours are 10am to 3pm Sydney time, and the rest of your day is yours to plan. Every engineer ships to production in their first two weeks.

## What we offer

- $135k-$150k base plus super.
- Work from anywhere in Australia, with a $1,500 home office budget.
- Two team weeks a year, travel paid.
- 20 days of leave, plus your birthday off.
- A yearly learning budget of $1,500.

## How to apply

Send your resume through our careers page. Skip the cover letter. Instead, answer one question in a few sentences: what is the slowest query you have fixed, and how did you find it?

Casey Park from our talent team will be in touch within five working days. The process is a call with Casey, a system design chat, and a code review exercise on a real pull request.
