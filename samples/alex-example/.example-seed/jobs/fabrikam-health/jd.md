# LLM Engineer at Fabrikam Health

Remote Australia · Full time · $125k-$145k + super

## About us

Fabrikam Health makes practice software for allied health clinics: physiotherapists, psychologists, speech pathologists and dietitians. Over 4,000 clinicians use it to book patients, write notes and send invoices.

Clinicians tell us note writing is the worst part of their day. Many spend an hour after work catching up. We are adding note drafting from session recordings, so a clinician can review a draft note instead of writing one from scratch.

## The role

You will join our AI squad of three engineers and a clinical lead, who is a practising physiotherapist. You will own the pipeline from recording to draft note, and the checks that keep each note accurate. Clinicians must always review and approve every note, and the system must make that review quick and safe.

## You'll

- Build the pipeline from transcript to draft note, with checks for missing details.
- Measure accuracy with clinicians and improve it every sprint.
- Design the review screen with our designers, so errors are easy to spot.
- Keep patient data inside our approved systems.
- Write clear notes on what changed and why, for our clinical safety review.

## You'll have

- Python and an LLM API in production.
- Experience with speech-to-text output.
- Evaluation with human review.

## Nice to have

- Health or other sensitive data.
- Summarisation of long or messy text.
- Experience writing for clinical or compliance reviewers.

## How we work

We are fully remote, with team members in four states. We work in two-week sprints, and every sprint ends with a demo to a group of clinicians from real clinics. Their feedback decides what we do next.
