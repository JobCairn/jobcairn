# Conversational AI Developer at Kestrel Robotics

Remote Australia · Full time · $120k-$140k

## About Kestrel

Kestrel builds warehouse robots that work beside people. Our carts follow pickers through the aisles, carry the load and tell them where to go next. Staff talk to them by voice and chat, often while wearing gloves and a headset, in a warehouse that is anything but quiet. We are 120 people, with engineering spread across Australia and a test warehouse in Sampletown.

## The role

Our voice assistant is the main way staff work with a robot, and today it only knows a few fixed commands. We want it to understand natural requests, ask a short question when it is unsure, and never send a picker to the wrong aisle. You will be the second developer on the conversational team, working with our speech engineers and robotics team.

## You'll

- Design voice and chat assistants that guide pickers through tasks.
- Write and test prompts, tools and fallbacks for noisy environments.
- Work with our speech and robotics teams on latency.
- Turn recordings from real shifts into test cases.
- Visit the test warehouse a few times a year to see the assistant in use.

## You'll need

- TypeScript and an LLM API in production.
- Experience with speech-to-text or text-to-speech APIs.
- Prompt design with tests.

## Nice to have

- Work on products used by frontline or shift workers.
- Experience with real-time systems or robotics.
- A second language. Many of our users speak English as a second language.

## What a good week looks like

You ship a prompt change on Tuesday after it passes the shift-recording tests. On Thursday you pair with a speech engineer to cut half a second off the reply time. On Friday you watch a short video from a customer site and add three new test cases.

## How to apply

Apply through LinkedIn or our careers page with your resume. Add a link to anything you have built with voice or chat, even a small project. Our hiring lead will reply within a week. The process is a call, a pairing session on a prompt with tests, and a chat with the robotics team.
