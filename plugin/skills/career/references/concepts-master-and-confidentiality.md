# Core Concepts: Master vs. Tailored, and Confidentiality

**Master vs. tailored.** Keep two tiers. The **master** is the complete,
unabridged history — every role, project, and metric, no length limit. It's the
source of truth; never send it to an employer. A **tailored** resume is a fresh
1–2 page version derived from the master for one specific posting: lead with the
most relevant experience, integrate the posting's keywords, cut the rest.
Generate a new tailored version per application; never edit the master to fit a
single job.

**Confidentiality comes first.** These docs describe *what the user did and the
skills they demonstrated* — not their employer's intellectual property. When
extracting from any source (Workflow 4), describe accomplishments, impact,
skills, and public/shareable metrics, but never copy proprietary code,
strategies, or processes verbatim, and never surface internal project codenames,
client names, credentials, internal URLs, or unpublished/confidential work. When
in doubt, abstract it: "Led go-to-market for an enterprise product launch," not
"Led the ProjectX launch targeting CompanyY's customers." This protects the user
as much as their former employer.
