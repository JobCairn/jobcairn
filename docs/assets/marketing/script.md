# Marketing video scripts

Narration for the website videos and the in-app guide videos. Every video is a screen recording of the example
workspace (Alex Example, all made up), read by the app's own AI voice (Supertonic, "Maya", warm and calm). A soft
music bed made by the app plays under the voice and drops when Alex is spoken about. Every frame carries the "AI
voice" mark, and every video opens and closes with "An example job search. Every person and company shown is made up."

Each line below is one beat: what the voice says, and what is on screen while it says it.

## What each claim rests on

- One folder on your computer, private papers locked: the welcome screen ("Everything stays in one folder on this
  computer") and Privacy's locked folders.
- Searches in your own Chrome: "Search in my Chrome" in Find jobs ("in your own Chrome, signed in as you, one page at
  a time").
- Search log and reports: the Searches screen.
- Fit and sponsor scores, sponsorship pinned: the Jobs table.
- Past sponsorship in company research: the company Sponsorship tab.
- Every line from your own history: the resume skill's no-fabrication rule ("every claim traces to a master doc"), and
  the claim check in the resume editor ("so every claim can be traced").
- PDF and Word: the resume editor's Download tab.
- Write with Claude, nothing sent without approval: the mail composer and Approvals.
- Undo after delete: the Undo toast and Settings > Recently deleted.
