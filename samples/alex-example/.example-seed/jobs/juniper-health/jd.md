# Senior Software Engineer (GenAI) at Juniper Health

Remote Australia · Full time · $150k-$170k + super

## About Juniper Health

Juniper Health runs a patient booking app used by 1.2 million Australians. Patients use it to find a GP, book a time, fill in forms and get reminders. Clinics use our dashboard to manage the day. We are 250 people, remote-first, with a small office in Sampletown for people who like one.

## The role

We are adding GenAI features to the booking app, and we want a senior engineer to lead them. You will set the technical direction for our first three features, guide two mid-level engineers, and work with our clinical safety lead on what we should and shouldn't build. This is a hands-on role. You will write code most days.

## What you'll do

- Lead the GenAI features in our booking app: summaries, smart search and reminders.
- Set up evaluation of model output with the clinical team.
- Review designs and code across the GenAI squad.
- Make sure patient data stays inside our approved systems at every step.
- Share what you learn with the wider engineering team through demos and short write-ups.

## About you

- 6+ years with Node.js and React.
- Shipped LLM features to real users.
- Comfortable leading technical decisions and explaining them.
- Care about privacy and safety in health products.

## Nice to have

- Experience with health or other sensitive data.
- React Native.
- Work with clinical or medical staff on product decisions.
