# Acme Corp: company research

A made-up company for the example workspace.

> **TL;DR**
> - Scheduling software for about 2,000 clinics. Profitable, steady growth.
> - Building an assistant that answers staff questions from clinic policies.
> - The ad says visa sponsorship is available, and two past ads said the same.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| What they make | Booking and rostering software for clinics |
| Size | About 350 people |
| Offices | Sampletown NSW (head office), remote staff across Australia |
| Engineering | About 60 engineers in 8 product teams |
| Stage | Private, profitable |

## What the AI team is building

The assistant answers questions like "Can I book a double session for a new patient?" from each clinic's own policies. The team is small: two engineers and a product manager. This role would be the third engineer.

## Sponsorship evidence

| Evidence | Where | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| "Visa sponsorship available for the right candidate." | This ad | Strong |
| Two earlier engineering ads offered sponsorship | Their careers page | Medium |
| Listed as an approved sponsor | Public sponsor list | Strong |

## Talking points

- Their assistant must cite clinic policies. Your evaluation suite checks answers against sources.
- They mention "simple actions, like moving a booking". Your support-reply agent is a close match.

## Questions to ask

1. How do you measure answer quality today?
2. Which actions will the assistant take first?
3. How does the team handle a bad answer in production?
