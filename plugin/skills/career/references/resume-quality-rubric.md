# Resume Quality Rubric (0–100)

Default rubric for Workflow 1. **If the project has
`{{DOCS_ROOT}}/09-resume-quality-rubric.md`, use that instead** — it reflects the
user's own standards and takes precedence. Treat these weights as a starting
point, not gospel; adjust with the user.

Score each category, sum to 100. Report the total plus the 1–2 lowest categories
as the priority fixes.

| Category | Weight | What earns full marks |
|----------|-------:|-----------------------|
| **Structure & completeness** | 20 | Header, headline (with target job title), executive summary, core competencies, reverse-chronological experience, education — all present and in sensible order. |
| **Bullet quality** | 25 | Every bullet is Action + Scope + Method + Outcome; quantified where evidence allows; no filler or duty-listing. |
| **Evidence & traceability** | 15 | Every claim maps to a master doc / cited conversation. No vague or unbacked assertions. |
| **Tone & clarity** | 10 | Confident, precise, outcomes-oriented; active voice; no clichés or hedging. |
| **ATS parseability** | 15 | Single-column, standard headings/fonts, no header/footer content, no meta text. (See `ats-formatting-standards.md`.) |
| **Relevance & focus** | 10 | Content prioritizes what matters for the target; length appropriate (tailored = 1–2 pages); weak/irrelevant items trimmed. |
| **Consistency** | 5 | Dates, titles, tense, and formatting uniform throughout; matches master timeline. |

## Scoring bands

- **90–100** — Submit-ready. Minor polish at most.
- **75–89** — Strong; a few targeted fixes needed.
- **60–74** — Solid base, meaningful gaps (usually bullets or ATS).
- **< 60** — Needs structural rework before it's competitive.

## How to report

1. Total score and band.
2. Category breakdown (only call out the ones that lost points).
3. The 1–3 highest-leverage fixes, with a concrete rewrite snippet each.
