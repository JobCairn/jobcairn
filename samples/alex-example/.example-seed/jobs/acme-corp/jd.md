# AI Engineer at Acme Corp

Sampletown NSW · Hybrid (2 days in the office) · Full time · $140k-$160k + super

## About us

Acme Corp makes scheduling software for 2,000 clinics across Australia and New Zealand. Physios, dentists and GPs use us to run their day: bookings, reminders, waitlists and payments. We are a team of about 300, and 60 of us are engineers.

Clinic staff ask the same questions all day. Can this patient move to Friday? What is our cancellation policy? Which forms does a new patient need? The answers live in each clinic's own policies, and they differ from clinic to clinic.

## The role

We are building an assistant that answers staff questions from each clinic's own policies, and takes simple actions when asked. It is in a pilot with 40 clinics. You will join a team of four engineers, a designer and a product manager, and help take it to every clinic this year.

## What you'll do

- Build LLM agents that answer questions and take simple actions, like moving a booking.
- Own retrieval: chunking, embeddings, reranking and citations.
- Set up evaluation so we know when a change makes answers worse.
- Work with product and design on how the assistant shows its sources.
- Look at real questions from the pilot each week and decide what to fix next.

## What you'll bring

- 3+ years building software in Python or TypeScript.
- Hands-on work with LLM APIs and retrieval (RAG) in production.
- A habit of measuring quality with test sets, not gut feel.
- Clear writing. You will explain trade-offs to non-engineers.

## Nice to have

- Postgres and pgvector.
- Agent frameworks or tool calling.
- Healthcare or scheduling products.
