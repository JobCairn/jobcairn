# AI Engineer, Search Quality at Example Labs

Remote Australia · Full time · $130k-$150k + super

## About Example Labs

Example Labs builds document search for law firms. Lawyers use us to find the right clause, case or email in matters that run to hundreds of thousands of pages, and to get a short answer with the sources attached. We are 70 people, fully remote across Australia.

## The role

Search that is almost right is not good enough for a lawyer. This new role owns the quality of our search and our answers. You will decide how we measure quality, build the test sets with our in-house legal experts, and lead the work to improve the numbers. You will sit between the search engineers and the legal team, and both will rely on you.

## You'll

- Build test sets with our legal experts and run them on every release.
- Tune hybrid search and reranking.
- Find where answers go wrong and fix the cause, not the symptom.
- Report quality to the whole company each month in plain language.
- Help customers' lawyers understand why a result ranked where it did.

## You have

- 3+ years of Python or TypeScript.
- Experience measuring search or answer quality.
- Retrieval in production.

## Nice to have

- Learning to rank, or other ranking models.
- Work with long or formal documents, such as contracts or policies.
- Experience labelling data with domain experts.

## Your first three months

- Month one: learn the product, read a hundred real searches, and meet the legal team.
- Month two: ship the first shared test set and a quality dashboard.
- Month three: lead your first improvement from test set to release.

## What we offer

- $130k-$150k base plus super.
- Remote anywhere in Australia, with core hours of 10am to 3pm Sydney time.
- Two team weeks a year, travel paid.
- A $1,500 home office budget and a $1,500 yearly learning budget.
- 20 days of leave, plus your birthday off.

## How to apply

Apply through our careers page with your resume. In the short answer field, tell us about a time you measured quality and the number changed your mind.

Casey Park from our talent team will reply within five working days. You'll then meet our Head of Search, do a short exercise on ranking a small set of results, and meet two of our legal experts.
