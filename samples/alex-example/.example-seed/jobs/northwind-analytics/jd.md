# AI Platform Engineer at Northwind Analytics

Remote Australia · Full time · $135k-$155k + super

## About Northwind

Northwind runs analytics for 300 retail stores across Australia. Store managers use our tools to plan stock, staff rosters and promotions. We are 700 people, and our data and engineering group is about 120 of them.

## The role

Six product teams now want to ship LLM features, and each one has started building the same pieces: a way to call models, store prompts, run evaluations and track cost. We are building a platform so every team can ship LLM features safely, without each team starting from scratch. You will be one of three engineers on the new AI Platform team.

## You'll

- Build shared services: prompt registry, model gateway, evaluation runs and cost tracking.
- Help product teams move LLM prototypes to production.
- Run the platform on our Kubernetes clusters, with good dashboards and alerts.
- Set sensible defaults for logging, privacy and spend limits.
- Write the guides that help other teams use the platform on their own.

## You have

- 4+ years of platform or backend engineering.
- Kubernetes in production.
- Experience running LLM APIs at scale.

## Nice to have

- Infrastructure as code.
- Building internal tools or platforms other engineers use.
- Retail or supply chain data.

## How we work

We are remote-first, with teams in five states. Platform teams treat other engineers as customers: we run office hours, keep a public roadmap, and measure success by how many teams ship on the platform. We are on call one week in five.

## What we offer

- $135k-$155k base plus super.
- Remote anywhere in Australia, with a yearly all-hands in person.
- 10% off at all our partner stores.
- $2,000 learning budget and conference leave.
- Paid parental leave and flexible hours.

## Work rights

Visa holders are welcome. We have sponsored engineers before.

## How to apply

Apply on LinkedIn or our careers site. Include your resume and a few lines about a platform or shared service you built and how other teams used it. The process is a recruiter call, a platform design interview, a Kubernetes troubleshooting session, and a chat with the team.
