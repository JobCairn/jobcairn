# Asset packs (bundled, offline)

Built once by `packages/video/scripts/build-packs.mjs`; licences in NOTICE.md.

## From the workspace

The media stage adds, when they exist: the company logo (`companies/<slug>/logo.svg|png|jpg|webp`, or in
`research/` or `assets/` there) on the title and company card, with a slow push-in, and the job board mark
the app bundles. Never write a picture path into spec.json.
