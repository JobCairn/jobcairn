# Applied AI Engineer at Contoso AI

Sampletown NSW · Hybrid · Full time · $145k-$165k + super

## Hi, we're Contoso AI

Contoso AI builds assistants for insurance claims teams. A claim can come with 50 documents: photos, invoices, medical reports and emails. Our agents read them, fill in the claim system, and draft the letters that go back to the customer. Claims staff check the work and send it. Small team, real customers, fast releases.

We are 30 people. Five insurers use us today, and we closed our Series A last year.

## The work

- Build agents that read claim documents, fill forms and draft letters.
- Own the evaluation set for each agent and report quality every week.
- Talk to claims staff to find where the agents get stuck.
- Ship to production every week, with a release note our customers can read.
- Help choose models and keep the cost of each claim down.

## You bring

- 3+ years of Python or TypeScript.
- Shipped at least one LLM feature to production.
- Retrieval and evaluation experience.

## Nice to have
