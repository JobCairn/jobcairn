# Applied AI Engineer at Harbourline Bank

Sampletown NSW · Onsite · Full time · Salary on application

## About Harbourline

Harbourline Bank is a customer-owned bank with 600,000 members and branches across regional New South Wales. We have been around for over a century, and we are proud of our customer service scores. We want to keep them high as more members contact us online.

## The team

Our new Applied AI team sits inside Customer Service Technology. It has three engineers today and a product owner who has worked in our contact centre for ten years. The team's job is to bring retrieval and document AI into the bank's customer service tools, safely and in a way our risk teams can stand behind.

## Responsibilities

- Build retrieval over product documents and past cases, so service staff find the right answer faster.
- Extract fields from forms with document AI, starting with hardship and home loan forms.
- Work with risk teams on model controls and audit trails.
- Measure accuracy before and after each release, and write it up for the model risk committee.
- Support the service teams who use what you build, including time on the contact centre floor.

## Requirements

- Python, SQL and an LLM API.
- Experience in a regulated industry.
- Clear documentation habits.
- 3+ years of software engineering.

## Desirable

- Document AI or OCR in production.
- Experience presenting technical results to risk or compliance staff.
- Banking or insurance knowledge.

## Working here

This role is based in our Sampletown head office five days a week. Our engineering teams sit next to the contact centre, and we think that is a strength. You will hear what members need every day.

## What we offer

- A competitive salary, discussed at the first interview, plus super.
- Discounted home loan rates and fee-free banking.
- 4 weeks of leave, plus a paid volunteering day.
- Study support for relevant courses.
- A health and wellbeing program for you and your family.

## How to apply

Apply on Indeed or through our careers site with your resume and a cover letter. Applications close in three weeks. We review them as they arrive, so apply early.

Our process has three steps: a call with our talent team, a technical interview with the Applied AI team, and a final interview with the head of Customer Service Technology. Final candidates complete background and reference checks.
