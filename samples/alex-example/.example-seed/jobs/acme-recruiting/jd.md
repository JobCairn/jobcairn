# AI Developer (via Acme Recruiting)

Sampletown NSW · Onsite · 6-month contract, possible extension · Daily rate

## About Acme Recruiting

Acme Recruiting places software and data people with government and enterprise clients in New South Wales. We have worked with this client for six years, and most of our contractors stay on past their first contract.

## About the client

Our client is a state government agency that manages licences and permits. Its staff answer thousands of questions a month from a library of policy documents, some of them hundreds of pages long. The team wants an internal chatbot that finds the right policy and quotes it, so staff can answer faster and more consistently.

## The role

You will join a small delivery team of three developers, a business analyst and a product owner from the agency. The first version is a pilot for one service centre. If it works, it rolls out to every centre next year.

## Responsibilities

- Build an internal chatbot over policy documents, with answers that quote their source.
- Set up retrieval over long documents: parsing, chunking and search.
- Write tests that check answers against a list of known questions.
- Work with the business analyst to collect questions from staff.
- Document the system so the agency's own team can run it after the contract.

## Requirements

- Python and an LLM API.
- Experience with retrieval over long documents.
- 3+ years of professional software development.
- Clear communication with non-technical people.
- Must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident. A police check applies before you start.

## Nice to have

- Work in government or another regulated setting.
- Azure or another cloud the client already uses.
