# LinkedIn Profile & Adjacent Outputs (Workflow 7)

Same evidence, same accuracy and anti-AI-slop rules as resumes (see
`writing-style-and-verbs.md`), but LinkedIn is more conversational and
search-driven, and the adjacent outputs (cover letter, interview prep) reuse the
master docs. Everything must still trace to evidence — no fabrication, and honor
the confidentiality mandate.

## LinkedIn profile

Build from the master docs; a resume already tailored for a target role is a good
starting point.

**Headline (≤220 chars).** Pattern: `Role | Value proposition | Notable proof`.
Front-load the exact job-title keywords the user is targeting.
- e.g. "Senior Product Manager | AI Tools 0→1 | Ex-Google, Stanford MBA"

**About (≤2,600 chars), first person, 3–5 short paragraphs.**
1. What you do now and your expertise.
2. Notable achievements with specific metrics.
3. Your approach/philosophy.
4. What drives you.
5. Optional call to action ("Let's connect if…").
Keep sentences short — the anti-AI-slop rules apply here too.

**Experience descriptions.** One opener sentence on scope, then 3–5 achievement
bullets (can be slightly richer than the resume). Optional line on tools/team.

**Skills.** 20–30 total; order matters because LinkedIn surfaces the top 3. Lead
with the skills most central to the target role; mix hard and soft skills; use
industry-standard terms and exact target job titles.

**SEO tips to pass along:** include target keywords 3–4 times naturally; keep the
profile 100% complete; custom URL; endorsements on the top skills.

## Cover letter

Short (250–350 words), specific, same voice as the resume. Structure:
1. Hook — why this company/role, referencing something real about them.
2. Proof — 2–3 achievements from the master docs that map to their top needs.
3. Fit — the throughline connecting the candidate to this role.
4. Close — direct, confident call to action.
Address any resume gap the user chose not to fabricate ("here's the adjacent
experience that covers X").

## STAR interview prep

For a target role, turn the strongest resume bullets into STAR stories the user
can tell out loud:
- **Situation / Task** — the context and what was at stake.
- **Action** — what the user specifically did.
- **Result** — the quantified outcome, plus what they'd do differently.
Pick 5–8 stories covering leadership, conflict, failure/learning, and biggest
impact. Keep each tellable in under two minutes.

## Cold outreach (recruiters / hiring managers)

3–4 sentences: who you are + one standout proof point, why this role/company
specifically, a low-friction ask (a short call). No filler, no flattery padding.

## Output locations

Save under the job folder or a profile folder, e.g.
`companies/<slug>/jobs/[id]/` for role-specific letters, or a
stable `linkedin.md` for the profile. Confirm the path with the user.
